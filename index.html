01 / What is quantum here?

Every graph link carries an exact normalized bipartite pure state, cos θ |00⟩ + sin θ |11⟩. Reduced density matrices yield the link mutual information I = 2H₂(cos²θ) without a many-body state-vector explosion.

02 / What is geometry here?

A stipulated monotone map turns mutual information into edge lengths. Triangle angles and interior angle defects produce a discrete curvature proxy. This mapping is not uniquely derived or experimentally validated.

03 / What remains unresolved?

No dynamical metric, graviton, diffeomorphism constraint, Einstein–Hilbert limit, Newtonian limit, or experimentally supported quantum-gravity prediction has been derived. EFMW is an independently testable hypothesis in this app.
